jobpipe privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

jobpipe is a personal job-search tool. Its only user is its author, Teja Papadasu, who signs in with their own Google account. This page explains what Google data it uses and what happens to it.

Google data jobpipe uses

  • Gmail, read-only. jobpipe reads only recent job-alert emails from the senders listed in its settings, such as LinkedIn job alerts. From each, it takes the jobs' company, title, location and link. It never sends, changes or deletes email, and it does not keep copies of emails.
  • Google Drive, only its own files. jobpipe can see only the files it created itself: one Google Sheet listing jobs and one folder of resume PDFs. It cannot see any other file in Drive.

How the data is used

  • Job details go into the owner's own Google Sheet, and draft resumes into the owner's own Drive folder.
  • To check how well a job fits and to draft a resume, jobpipe sends job details (company, title, and the posting's text from the job's public page) to Anthropic's Claude, through the owner's own Claude account.

Where it is stored

In the owner's Google Sheet and Drive, plus a cache of job details and settings on the owner's computer and in the owner's private GitHub repository. Google sign-in tokens are kept in the owner's local settings and as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. jobpipe's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The Sheet and folder jobpipe created can be deleted from Google Drive like any other file.
