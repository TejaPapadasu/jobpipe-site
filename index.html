jobpipe

A personal job-search tool

jobpipe is a tool that Teja Papadasu built and uses for their own job search. It collects job postings from public company job boards and from job-alert emails in Teja's own Gmail, checks them against Teja's own profile, and prepares draft resumes in Teja's own Google Drive and Google Sheet.

Teja reviews everything and applies to jobs by hand. jobpipe never submits applications or contacts employers.

It is not a public service: it has one user, its author, who signs in with their own Google account.
